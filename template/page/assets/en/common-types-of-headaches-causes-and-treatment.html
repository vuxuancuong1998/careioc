Common Types of Headaches – Causes & Effective Treatments

Have you ever had a persistent headache, migraine, or a headache when the weather changes? Each type of headache has different causes and symptoms, and understanding the symptoms will help you choose a more effective way to relieve the pain.

The man holds his head, showing pain in the temples – a symptom of a headache.

Let's find out more details with Dai Phuoc General Clinic to protect your health and your loved ones!

Primary headaches

These are often persistent, recurring types of pain.

1. Tension Headache - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Patients often feel tension, pain on both sides of the head or forehead, dull pain.
  • Often occurs when stressed, anxious or sitting in the wrong position.
  • Rarely accompanied by nausea or sensitivity to light.

Treatment:

  • Use pain relievers: Paracetamol, Ibuprofen.
  • Relax with meditation, yoga, or massage.
  • Drink plenty of water and avoid stress.

2. Migraine Headache - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Often has family factors, women are more affected than men.
  • Often have migraines, feeling of pulse in both temples.
  • Sensitive to light, sound, possibly accompanied by nausea, blurred vision.
  • May appear from a few hours to a few days.

Treatment:

  • Rest in a quiet, dimly lit space.
  • Don't eat MSG, don't drink coffee. Drink enough water.
  • Use pain relievers - pain relievers: Ibuprofen, Naproxen.
  • Prophylactic treatment: Flunarizine, Bisoprolol.
  • Use specific drugs: Triptan group drugs.

3. Cluster Headache - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Often has a family factor, men are more affected than women.
  • Pain around the eyes, temples, usually occurs on one side.
  • Occurs in episodes, accompanied by watery eyes and stuffy nose.

Treatment:

  • Breathe 100% oxygen through a mask.
  • Medications: Sumatriptan, Zolmitriptan.

Secondary headaches

This often occurs due to other causes, so the pain is often sudden and the patient feels different from previous pains.

1. Hypertension Headache - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Pain in the back of the neck, spreading to the top of the head.
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, shortness of breath.

Treatment:

  • Control blood pressure with medication: Amlodipine, Lisinopril.
  • Limit salt, fat, exercise.

2. Headache due to Ear, Nose, Throat or Eye Diseases - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Most painful in the forehead, sides of the nose or eye sockets, red eyes...
  • Pain is worse when bending over or changing position.
  • Stuffy nose, runny nose, possibly with fever.
  • With blurred vision

Treatment:

  • Rinse nose with saline.
  • Use anti-inflammatory nasal sprays (Fluticasone, Mometasone).
  • Take antibiotics as prescribed by your doctor.

3. Headache due to neurological diseases (brain tumor, stroke, meningitis,...) - Symptoms & Treatment

Symptoms:

  • Appear suddenly.

  • Headache after strenuous activity or exertion.

  • Headache when using mouth muscles.

  • Increased intensity when coughing.

  • Sudden headache with symptoms such as stiff neck, change in consciousness...

Treatment:

If symptoms appear suddenly, the pain is severe, or the type of pain is different from previous headaches, you NEED TO BE EXAMINED IMMEDIATELY .

When to see a doctor?

You should see a doctor if you experience the following symptoms:

  • Headache with loss of consciousness, paralysis, difficulty speaking.
  • Headache does not subside despite medication.
  • Headache with high fever, persistent nausea.
