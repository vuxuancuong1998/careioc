Make an appointment

Note: Please fill in complete patient information

Check in instructions

Step 1: Get a serial number

- Press the machine to get a number near the cashier counter

- Sit in a chair and follow the order number

Step 2: Make a medical record & Payment

- Make medical records

- Pay for medical examination or requested services

Step 3: Measure vital signs

- Medical staff will measure weight, height, blood pressure,...

Step 4: Doctor examination and consultation

- The doctor will ask about medical history, examine, and give clinical instructions (if any).

Step 5: Payment

- Pay paraclinical service fee

Step 6: Perform paraclinical indications

- Perform functions: Blood test, X-ray, Endoscopy, Ultrasound

- Get results

Step 7: Doctor examines, reads and explains the results

- Bring all paraclinical results for the doctor to read the results, diagnose, advise, prescribe,...

Step 8: Receive medication

- Health insurance beneficiaries: Pay the cost of medicine (if co-payment occurs), sign the confirmation of health insurance medical examination records at the cashier. Receive medicine at the pharmacy counter.

- Hospital fee: Pay medicine fee at ground floor cashier. Receive medicine at pharmacy counter.
