Department of Gastrointestinal Endoscopy

Detailed description

Gastrointestinal diseases are a common group of diseases in the community, which can occur at any age, from children to the elderly. Gastrointestinal diseases include many types of diseases from the esophagus, stomach, duodenum, liver, gallbladder, pancreas to the colon and anus. There are many methods to diagnose diseases from the digestive tract and currently, digestive endoscopy is an effective method to diagnose and treat diseases of the digestive tract, especially early diagnosis of digestive tract cancers such as the esophagus, stomach and colon.

The Department of Gastrointestinal Endoscopy at Dai Phuoc General Clinic gathers leading doctors in the field of Gastrointestinal Endoscopy from major hospitals and medical universities in Ho Chi Minh City with a modern endoscopy system and an automatic endoscopy sterilization system that meets international standards. Here, we specialize in:

  • Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy (esophagus, stomach, duodenum): diagnosis and treatment, polyp resection < 2cm
  • Lower gastrointestinal endoscopy (Colon): diagnosis and treatment, polyp resection < 2cm
  • Color endoscopy, magnification for early cancer diagnosis (Esophagus, stomach, colon, rectum)

Some diseases require endoscopy:

  • Gastroesophageal reflux
  • Gastritis
  • Suspected gastrointestinal bleeding
  • Family history of gastrointestinal cancer
  • Suspected gastrointestinal cancers such as esophageal, stomach, colon...
  • Colorectal polyps.
  • Digestive disorders
  • Suspected foreign body in the digestive tract
  • Colitis
  • Irritable bowel syndrome
  • Hemorrhoids and anal canal diseases
